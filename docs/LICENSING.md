# 使用、发行与商业授权 / Use, distribution, and commercial licensing

本页解释许可场景，不代替 [GPLv3](../LICENSE)、[署名条款](../NOTICE)或单独签署的商业协议。

| 场景 | 规则 |
| --- | --- |
| 个人使用或私人修改 | 不收取许可费用，无需公开修改源码；私人修改署名不因此要求购买授权 |
| 公司内部使用或修改，不向组织外发行 | 同上；不因公司营利而收取使用许可费用 |
| 原版或二开版对外发行，包括收费售卖 | 履行 GPL，向接收者提供对应源码，保留版权、许可证及合理界面署名，注明修改，不缩小接收者权利 |
| 保留署名，但想闭源发行 | 保留署名不能代替 GPL 源码义务；须取得覆盖相关材料的单独商业授权 |
| 对外发行时想移除规定的项目声明 | 须事先取得有权授权者的书面许可；可以通过仓库洽谈付费授权 |

发行版的“关于”或法律声明须按 NOTICE 展示项目地址及版权声明，并提供许可证入口。允许不同界面设计和含义相同的本地化呈现，不要求保留原 Logo。公司与个人适用相同规则。

商业授权只免除书面约定范围内、授权方有权免除的义务；第三方依赖仍须遵守各自许可证。收费售卖且完整履行 GPL，本身不要求购买商业授权。

发现违规发行时，先核对适用许可证和证据，再要求恢复合规或停止违规发行；是否另行商业授权由双方协商。违规本身不自动产生一笔约定的授权费。许可终止及恢复按 GPL 第 8 条处理。

贡献者按 [CLA](../CLA.md)授予项目所有人商业再授权权利，保留著作权及公开贡献记录。项目所有人无需就已授权贡献逐次征求同意或另付许可费。扩大 CLA 权利范围须另行接受；CLA 工作流自动核验，未签署时阻塞 PR 合并及打包。

## English

This explanation does not replace [GPLv3](../LICENSE), the [attribution notice](../NOTICE), or a separately signed commercial agreement.

| Scenario | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Personal use or private modification | No license fee or publication of changes is required; private attribution changes alone do not require a paid license |
| Internal company use or modification without external conveyance | The same rules apply; operating a for-profit company alone does not require a paid license |
| Conveying original or modified versions, including selling copies | Comply with GPL, provide corresponding source to recipients, preserve copyright and license notices and reasonable interface attribution, identify modifications, and do not narrow recipients' rights |
| Keeping attribution but seeking closed-source distribution | Attribution alone does not replace GPL source obligations; obtain a separate commercial license covering the relevant material |
| Removing required project notices in a conveyed version | Obtain prior written permission from a party entitled to grant it; paid authorization may be negotiated through the repository |

Conveyed interactive versions must display the project URL and copyright notice and provide license access in their About or Legal Notices interface as specified in NOTICE. Different designs and equivalent localized notices are permitted; the original logo is not required. Individuals and companies follow the same rules.

Commercial exceptions cover only obligations and rights expressly covered by the written agreement and controlled by the grantor. Third-party dependency licenses still apply. Selling copies while complying with GPL does not by itself require a commercial license.

For suspected noncompliant distribution, verify the applicable license and evidence, then request compliance or cessation. A commercial agreement may be negotiated; a violation does not automatically create an agreed license fee. GPL section 8 governs license termination and reinstatement.

Under the [CLA](../CLA.md), contributors retain copyright and public contribution credit while granting the Project Owner commercial relicensing rights without repeated consent or further license fees. An expanded CLA requires separate acceptance. The CLA workflow verifies acceptance and blocks PR merging and packaging when signatures are missing.
